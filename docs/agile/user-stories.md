# StyleSense AI — User Stories

### US-01: User Registration & Authentication
* **As a** fashion-conscious individual,
* **I want to** create a secure account and sign in with email and password,
* **So that** my personal wardrobe items and saved looks remain private and synced.

### US-02: Fashion Profiler Onboarding
* **As a** new user,
* **I want to** answer 4 quick questions about my favorite styles, colors, occasions, and fashion goals,
* **So that** StyleSense AI can personalize outfit generation to my aesthetic preferences.

### US-03: Digital Wardrobe Management
* **As a** user,
* **I want to** upload, view, edit, search, and categorize clothing garments in my digital closet,
* **So that** I have a single digital source of truth for everything I own.

### US-04: AI Outfit Generation
* **As a** user getting ready for a specific event (e.g. college farewell),
* **I want to** input the occasion, formality, and weather conditions,
* **So that** the system curates ranked outfit combinations with transparent compatibility scores.

### US-05: Conversational Fashion Bestie
* **As a** user seeking friendly styling advice,
* **I want to** chat with StyleSense like a fashionable best friend,
* **So that** I receive wardrobe-first suggestions, color coordination tips, and product recommendations.

### US-06: Travel Packing Capsule
* **As a** traveler heading to Manali for 5 days,
* **I want to** enter my destination, duration, and trip purpose,
* **So that** StyleSense generates an itemized packing checklist and coordinates travel looks from my existing closet.

### US-07: Virtual Try-On
* **As a** shopper,
* **I want to** upload my photo and preview how garments fit on my silhouette,
* **So that** I can visualize outfits with high visual confidence before purchasing.
